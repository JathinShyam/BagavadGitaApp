Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026

In short: Gita Jyoti does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

This Privacy Policy explains how 1001681804 Ontario Inc. ("we", "us") handles information in the mobile app Gita Jyoti: Bhagavad Gita (the "App") on iOS and Android.

Information we collect

None. The App has no user accounts and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts or any other personal information. We do not use analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking software, and the App does not send information about you to us or to anyone else.

Information stored on your device

To work, the App keeps a few things locally on your phone:

  • your reading progress, streak and the verses you have read;
  • verses you save;
  • your settings, such as theme, content language, reminder time and audio preferences.

This information never leaves your device. You can remove it at any time from Settings → Reset Reading Progress in the App, or by deleting the App.

Notifications

If you turn on daily reminders, the App schedules them with your phone's own notification system. Reminders are created on the device; no information is sent to a server to deliver them. You can turn them off in the App or in your phone's settings.

Sharing

When you choose to share a verse, the App creates an image on your device and hands it to the share sheet of your phone. What happens next is controlled by the app you share to, not by us.

Internet access

The App works offline. All verse text and audio are included in the App.

Children

The App is suitable for all ages. Because we collect no personal information, we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. If a future version of the App ever collects information, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
