Support

Questions, a problem, or a correction to a verse? Email hello@urbanhelper.ca and include your phone model and the app version (Settings → About). We usually reply within two working days.

Common questions

How do I change the language?
Settings → Appearance → Content language. Choose English, Telugu, Hindi or Tamil.

I'm not getting the daily reminder.
Check that reminders are on in Settings → Notifications, and that notifications for Gita Jyoti are allowed in your phone's settings. On Android, battery-saving modes can delay reminders.

Does the app need internet?
No. All verses and audio are included, and nothing you do is sent anywhere.

How do I start over?
Settings → Reset Reading Progress clears your progress and streak on this device.

Android home-screen widget
Open the app once, then long-press your home screen → Widgets → Gita Jyoti → Daily Verse.
