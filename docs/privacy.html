Overview

Bagavad Gita is a reading app. We respect your privacy.

Data We Collect

All data is stored locally on your device:

  • Reading progress – chapters and verses you've read
  • Saved verses – verses you bookmark
  • Settings – theme, notifications, auto-play preferences

We do not collect, transmit, or sell your personal data.

Notifications

If you enable daily verse notifications, we use your device's local notification system. No data is sent to our servers.

Third Parties

This app does not use analytics or third-party tracking.
